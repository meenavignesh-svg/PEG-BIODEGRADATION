BioHub PEG Biodegradation

Problem: PEG is used in medicines, cosmetics and industry. Heavier PEG can stay in water and soil longer before bacteria break it fully.

Our idea: Keep useful bacteria on a biochar and hydrogel bed so they stay in place longer, work on the waste, and move it step by step toward cleaner water.

About PEG

PEG dissolves in water and shows up in many products. Smaller PEG is easier for microbes to use. Larger PEG often needs to be cut shorter first. Lab tests under good conditions can show strong biodegradation.

Why a bed?

Biochar gives surface area. Hydrogel holds water and cells. Keeping bacteria on the bed can help them stay put and work longer than if they float free.

Why three bacteria?

One starts on the polymer, one uses the smaller bits, one helps with biofilm and enzymes. Papers on PEG often mention Pseudomonas and Sphingomonas. Our three names show a team idea for this demo. Lab work would choose the final strains.

What this is not

This page explains our idea for SIH. It is not a full plant design and we are not claiming measured removal numbers yet. Next work would be lab tests and scale planning.
