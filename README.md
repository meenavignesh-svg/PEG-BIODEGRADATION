# BioHub — PEG Biodegradation Prototype

**SIH 2026 · Clean & Green Technology**  
**Team:** Marge Organics

Interactive conceptual model of how **polyethylene glycol (PEG)** waste can move through a biochar–hydrogel support and a three-role microbial consortium toward a treated stream.

> Conceptual demonstration for Smart India Hackathon 2026 — **not** a validated plant design or measured performance claim.

### Process steps

1. **Polymer feed** — PEG-containing wastewater enters  
2. **Biochar–hydrogel** — porous wet support for cells  
3. **Microbial consortium** — three coordinated roles  
4. **Degradation path** — chains → oligomers → metabolites  
5. **Treated stream** — conceptual end point  

### Consortium (demo roles)

| Organism | Role in this model |
|----------|--------------------|
| *I. sakaiensis* | Polymer-facing / depolymerization role |
| *P. putida* | Intermediate consumption |
| *B. subtilis* | Biofilm and enzyme support |

Real PEG literature often highlights *Pseudomonas* and *Sphingomonas* specialists; the three strains here illustrate team design for the demo.
